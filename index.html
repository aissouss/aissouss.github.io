About

I graduated in 2026 with a bachelor's degree in Software Engineering & Information Systems from Université Internationale de Tunis.

For my final-year internship at Scalnyx, I designed and built ScalTerminal, an AI agent that lets users work with the Scalnyx platform through natural-language requests. The agent turns questions into REST API calls, keeps conversation memory, and comes with Streamlit and command-line interfaces and a pytest test suite. The project was graded Excellent by the jury.

I started with a year of health studies (PASS) at Université Paris Cité before switching to computer science. I'm interested in data analytics and in building software that turns data and APIs into useful tools.
